## Scope and public surface

The three main Issue 5 API groups are the reusable segment relation, the
two-step demonstration VM, and the theorem that combines extracted segments.
Their supporting public declarations are listed explicitly here, as required
by I5:

- Bus data and system parameters: `BusState`, `BusState.ofState`, `HashCall`,
  `HashCall.ofStates`, `SegmentBus`, `HashChip`, `StepAux`, `SegmentStmt`,
  `InnerStepStmt`, `SegmentTrace`, `SegmentWitness`, and `System`.
- Step and proof-system definitions: `busCommitment`, `keccakChip`,
  `poseidonChip`, `rangeChip`, `stepBus`, `stepWithBus`, `RInnerStep`,
  `ASInnerStep`, `RInnerKeccak`, `RInnerPoseidon`, `RInnerRange`,
  `ASInnerKeccak`, `ASInnerPoseidon`, `ASInnerRange`, `RSegment`, `ASSegment`,
  `Assumptions`, and `segmentValid`. `RInnerChip` is the shared definition used
  by the three named chip relations. Keeping it public ensures that readers can
  name every definition on which those public relations depend.
- Two-layer connection definitions: `TwoStepSystem`, `TwoStepSystem.toTwoStep`,
  `TwoStepSystem.Assumptions`, `TwoStepSystem.Execution`, `Execution.trace`,
  `Execution.Valid`, and `TwoStepSystem.toZkVM`.
- Connecting theorems: `System.stepWithBus_committedOperation`,
  `System.segment_extract`, `TwoStepSystem.busBridge`,
  `TwoStepSystem.execution_extract`, and `TwoStepSystem.cte`.

These declarations expose the witness types and paper relations that the
headline theorems mention. Making them private would hide the statements that
Benedikt must compare with the paper. No new security definition, trace-joining
algorithm, committed-step relation, or plain-step relation is introduced:
`KnowledgeSound`, `CTE`, `concatTrace`, `chain_flatten`,
`ISA.System.committedStep`, and `ISA.System.stepPlain` are reused.
